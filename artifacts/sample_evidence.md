# SpectrumLens: sample signal evidence (held-out `test` windows)

Each example is one recorded 4096-sample complex baseband window (1 MS/s, 16 sub-bands of 62.5 kHz) from the held-out `test` split. For every relevant sub-band, SpectrumLens reports *why* each detector declared it occupied or vacant: the measured PSD statistic, the noise floor it was compared against, the margin to the threshold and a confidence. The learned detector reports its posterior and the per-feature contributions to its logit. Generated by `npm start`; numbers match `artifacts/benchmark_report.json`.

Detectors:
- **Baseline Energy (Gen 0)**: band-mean PSD, rectangular taper, single periodogram, fixed threshold -57.00 dB/Hz on an assumed floor.
- **Self-Evolved Window (Gen 3)**: `{"bandStatistic":"peak","subWindows":4,"integration":"average","taper":"rectangular","noiseFloor":{"kind":"median"},"threshold":{"kind":"relative","marginDb":5.879}}`
- **Learned Feature Detector**: L2 logistic regression on 9 spectral features, fitted on 4800 dev cells (410 occupied); decision threshold p > 0.178.
